## Business description (paste ke form)
Softly is a polite collections desk for solo freelancers and small service businesses.
The problem: solo operators lose real cash chasing unpaid invoices because asking for money feels awkward — they delay the follow-up, the invoice ages, and the client relationship gets tense. It is consistently one of the loudest pain points in r/smallbusiness, r/Freelancers and r/Entrepreneur ("rant: customers not paying invoices", "chasing clients for payment").
The solution: Softly schedules the follow-up rhythm automatically (reminder 2 days before the due date, a nudge on the due date, then day +3, +7, +14, and a firm final notice) and writes the message for you in three tones — Soft ("just confirming this came through"), Warm (friendly but specific) and Firm (clear, final notice). The dashboard tracks total outstanding, aging buckets (Not due / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61+ days) and a "Nudge today" queue so the freelancer never has to decide when or how to ask.
Everything was built with Bolt (bolt.new) during Builders Week.
